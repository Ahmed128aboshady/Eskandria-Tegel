01 Heritage

From the Mediterranean Sea to Germany

ANCIENT HARBOUR, MODERN HEART.

Named after Alexandria—Egypt’s legendary second city, the "Bride of the Mediterranean" founded by Alexander the Great. Home to the Pharos Lighthouse, one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World. We brought that coastal soul, the scent of charcoal, freshly ground cumin, and seaside seafood recipes straight to Berlin Tegel. Here, authentic Egyptian street comfort meets an unexpected surprise: handmade European and Polish desserts.

03 The Craft

Fire, Earth & Patience

LAVA GRILL. CLAY OVEN.

Every fish is cleaned and seasoned to order. Every skewered kebab and lamb cutlet meets volcanic lava coals. No shortcuts, no compromises—just blazing heat, marinades passed down through generations, and the warmth of a true family table.

  1. 01

    The 12-Hour Marinade

    Fresh whole fish, calamari and meats are marinated in fresh lemon juice, garlic cloves, cumin and sea salt.

    Cold infuse · 12 Hours
  2. 02

    Lava Stone Sizzle

    High radiant heat seals the juices instantly. Kofta, Shish Tawook, and Lamb Chops get that signature smokiness.

    400°C · Lava Coals
  3. 03

    Slow Earthenware Bake

    Seafood, pasta béchamel and rice tajins slowly bubble in authentic clay pots to lock in coastal depth.

    Wood & Ceramic Bake
  4. 04

    The Feast Served Hot

    Brought sizzling straight to your table with steaming sayadia rice, freshly puffed baladi bread, and salads.

    Direct to Table
